Workshop topic and content

Workshop abstract

In practice, capable NLP reaches most settings where low-resource languages are used through small or efficient models—compressed, distilled, adapted with parameter-efficient methods, or trained compact from the start. Yet efficiency research is conducted almost entirely language-agnostically and evaluated almost entirely in English: what an efficient model loses in a morphologically rich, under-tokenised, or data-scarce language is essentially unmeasured.

FLoRes aims to promote research at this intersection and give it a dedicated home. The focus is primarily on written language, though studies of spoken language technologies are welcome.

02 / Topics

Topics of interest

01

Efficiency-induced degradation

Efficiency-induced degradation across languages, scripts, and tokenisations—its measurement, prediction, and mitigation.

02

Compression and distillation

What compression and distillation preserve or destroy in cross-lingual transfer.

03

Parameter-efficient adaptation

Parameter-efficient adaptation when the constraint is the target language rather than the task.

04

Compact models

Compact models trained from scratch for low-resource languages and language families.

05

Evaluation methodology

Evaluation methodology for efficient models beyond English.

06

Deployment studies

Deployment studies under real constraints—on-device use, limited connectivity, community-scale compute—including for spoken language technologies.
